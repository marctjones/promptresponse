## How to read these

- **coverage / invented** — against the PDF's own field list. Tier 1: the PDF states it.
  100% is the floor for an AcroForm import, not an achievement; it means nothing was dropped.
- **tau(decl)** — order against the AcroForm's declaration order. Usually 1.000, because
  the importer emits fields as declared — but *not* tautologically so, and `fed-i9` proves it
  at **0.276**. The importer groups prompts into one section per page (I-9: 51/38/39), and
  I-9's AcroForm declares its fields interleaved across pages, giving 2,943 inversions.
  Page grouping is the sensible behaviour; the number simply shows declaration order and
  emitted order are not the same thing on every form.
- **tau(geom)** — order against geometric reading order. The real signal, and the one a
  flat-form converter will be graded on, since it has no access to declaration order.
- **tooltips / cryptic** — how many labels are human-readable. This is the number the
  converter is meant to improve, and it is why #426 exists.
- **A present tooltip is not a good tooltip.** `ct-dmv-a25` reads 100% tooltips *and*
  100% cryptic: every field carries a `/TU`, and every one of them says `TextField1`.
  Tooltip coverage therefore overstates label quality, and #426's target population is
  better defined by the cryptic ratio than by absent tooltips.
