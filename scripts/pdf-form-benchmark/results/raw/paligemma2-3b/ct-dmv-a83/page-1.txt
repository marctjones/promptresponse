INSTRUCTIONS: The form below may be used to use a motor vehicle as an agent for the Commissioner of Motor Vehicles in connection with any debt transactions involving a motor vehicle registration or title.
Please note that this is not a substitute for your attorney's office and should be filed with the Commissioner of Motor Vehicles in conjunction with any other transaction involving a motor vehicle registration or title.
1. This form must be completed in its entirety. The form requires your signature, the signature of two witnesses, and the signature of an agent to perform transactions related to motor vehicles. Please read all instructions carefully before completing this form. 2. The Special Power of Attorney does not authorize your agent to perform transactions related to your motor vehicle operators' license or driver licenses in connection with any debt transaction involving a motor vehicle registration or title.
3. This Special Power of Attorney bearing original signatures must be shown and will be returned to you.
4. Please sign any other documents as well if you wish to use the special power of attorney for any Court of Probate for an accounting, as provided in subsection (b) of section 65a-173 of the Connecticut General Statutes.
