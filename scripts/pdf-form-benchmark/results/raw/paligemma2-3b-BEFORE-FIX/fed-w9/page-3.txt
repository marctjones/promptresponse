What is FATCA Reporting?
The Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) requires U.S. tax payers to report payments made to foreign persons on Form W-9. You can find more information about FATCA reporting. See Examples from FATCA reporting code, table and figure 1 for U.S. tax information.

If you are a foreign person on Form W-9, you are required to provide the name and tax information of the person on line 2b. If you are a foreign person with no income with which to compute the penalty for furnishing information, you must provide the name and tax information of the person on line 2b.
