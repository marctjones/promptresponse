# Standard Form 86 (2016) - section-organized source index

Source: U.S. Office of Personnel Management, Standard Form 86, revised November 2016 (136 pages).

The complete mechanically extracted text is retained in [sf86-2016-official-extracted.txt](sf86-2016-official-extracted.txt). This index deliberately preserves the official section identifiers, including 13A-C and 20A-C, rather than imposing a new numbering scheme. It is an audit artifact, not a replacement form.

## Instructions, notices, and certification material

The source contains several pages of introductory instructions, privacy/routine-use notices, and certifications before Section 1, and a final certification plus release material after Section 29. See the extracted text file lines 1-320 and 7982 onward.
