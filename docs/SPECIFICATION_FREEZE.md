# APR specification freeze

The APR 1.0-beta.6 format is frozen.  No change to APR semantics, the
normative schemas, conformance corpus, specification, or an implementation's
interpretation of the format may be proposed, read for action, designed,
implemented, reviewed, or merged unless a human explicitly unfreezes the
specification in the active task and names the permitted scope.

Examples, applications, SDKs, tests, and explanatory documentation may be
corrected to conform to the frozen format.  Those corrections must not imply a
new data member, a new meaning for an existing member, or a renderer behavior
that the frozen specification does not define.

This policy is deliberately stronger than ordinary contribution guidance: an
issue, comment, or roadmap item is not authorization to work on a possible
format evolution.  Explicit human approval is required before even considering
such an item.
